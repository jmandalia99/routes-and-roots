My dance journey

Where my roots are मूल

Bharatanatyam taught me discipline, rhythm, and how to tell a story without words. After a long break, I'm back in training.

Childhood
6 years

Trained from 1st to 6th standard and took the Gandharva Mahavidyalaya exams through Bruhad Gujarat Sangeet Samiti, Ahmedabad, reaching the Visharad Pratham level.

School years
Up to 10th standard

Kept dancing through school, performing prayer dances on stage.

2026
